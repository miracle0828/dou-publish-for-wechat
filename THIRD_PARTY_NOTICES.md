# Third-party notices

This plugin bundles `baoyu-md` and its runtime dependencies to provide Markdown rendering and inline styles. `baoyu-md` is published from the [baoyu-skills](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-skills) project. Its distributed source notes that parts are adapted from [doocs/md](https://github.com/doocs/md) under the WTFPL Version 2.

The dependency tree also includes open-source packages such as Marked, highlight.js, Juice, Unified and Remark. Their license texts and current versions are available through `package-lock.json` and their respective package repositories.
